You pull on your favorite chunky sweater, the one that's soft, familiar, and never asks anything hard of you, and for a moment everything feels fine. Then you catch your reflection in the hallway mirror on your way out the door, and something feels off. Not wrong exactly, just heavier than you meant to look. You didn't gain weight. You didn't change. What changed is a handful of small habits hiding inside the exact same sweater you've worn a hundred times this year, habits so ordinary you'd never think to question them.

This isn't about giving up the sweaters you love. It's about understanding why the softest, safest piece in your closet can quietly work against you after sixty, when your skin, your shoulders, and the way fabric moves over your body have all shifted in small ways nobody warned you about. Once you see these seven things, you'll never put on a sweater the same way again, and every fix here takes less than a minute, using things you likely already own.
