The seventh find is the smallest and cheapest one on this list, and it might do more work than anything else here: a leather belt with a simple metal buckle. Real leather molds gently to the body and defines the waist with a clean, confident line that plastic or faux leather can't replicate, since synthetic belts tend to sit stiffly rather than settling naturally against the body, and a good leather belt is often the single least expensive item on the thrift rack that instantly elevates an entire outfit around it.

Look for leather that feels supple rather than stiff when you bend it, and a buckle with real weight and a secure, well-fitted prong rather than a loose or wobbly one. This one belt works for practically every occasion you own, from a weekday at the office to a night out, simply by changing what buckle style you pair it with, a plain brushed metal buckle for daytime, something with a little more shine or detail for evening. It needs no other accessory alongside it, no matching bag or jewelry required, just a good leather belt cinched at the natural waist, quietly doing the work that a whole new outfit usually gets credit for.
