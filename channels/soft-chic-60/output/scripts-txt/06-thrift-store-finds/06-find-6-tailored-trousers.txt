The sixth find is a pair of tailored trousers in a wool or cotton blend, which matters more than most shoppers realize because natural fiber blends hold a crease and skim a changing midsection and hips in a way synthetic fabric never quite manages, no matter how well it's cut. A trouser made from cheap polyester will shine under light and cling in all the wrong places by early afternoon, while a wool blend keeps its structure and its crease from morning until night.

Check the tag for wool, cotton, or a blend of the two before you even check the fit, then look at the crease itself, a trouser that's been pressed and still holds a sharp line on the hanger is telling you something honest about the fabric underneath. For work, pair tailored trousers with a simple blouse for an instantly finished look. For a day out, the same trousers with a plain tee and blazer feel relaxed but still pulled together. And for evening, swap in a silk top and the trousers carry the whole outfit upward. A slim belt in a matching tone at the waist finishes the line without adding any bulk.
