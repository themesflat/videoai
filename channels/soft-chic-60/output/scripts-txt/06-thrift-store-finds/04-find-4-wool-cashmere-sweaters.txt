The fourth find is a wool or cashmere sweater, but only after you've actually checked the label, because this is one category where acrylic hides in plain sight dressed up as something finer. Real wool and cashmere hold their shape wash after wash and drape softly over a body that has changed shape over the years, skimming rather than clinging, while acrylic blends stretch out at the elbows and pill within a handful of wears no matter how gently you treat them.

Check the tag for wool or cashmere content before anything else, then stretch the cuff gently and watch whether it springs back into shape, a good sign the fibers are strong rather than synthetic filler. For work, a fine wool sweater over trousers looks quietly expensive without trying. For a casual day out, the same sweater with well-fitted jeans feels easy and put-together. And even at home, a soft cashmere sweater is polished enough that you won't flinch if a neighbor knocks unexpectedly. Once you bring it home, tuck a small cedar sachet into the drawer where you store it, a simple habit that keeps moths away and protects real wool fibers for years of wear still ahead of it.
