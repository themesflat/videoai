The fifth and strongest formula stacks two vertical lines on top of each other: a wide-leg pant with a visible vertical seam or subtle pinstripe, paired with a fitted turtleneck or mock-neck top that runs in one unbroken column straight up to the chin. A turtleneck eliminates the horizontal line a crew neck or v-neck creates across the chest, and a vertical seam down the pant leg gives the eye a literal line to follow from ankle to waist, so the two pieces work together instead of the top interrupting what the pants are already doing.

The reason this is the most powerful formula of the five is that it removes every horizontal interruption at once, no visible neckline, no visible waist break, no hem detail, just one continuous vertical read from chin to floor. For the office, a fine-knit charcoal turtleneck with pinstriped wide-leg trousers reads instantly sharp and tall. Running errands, a lightweight ribbed turtleneck with seamed wide-leg denim feels relaxed but still elongated. For evening, a fitted turtleneck in a rich jewel tone with wide-leg trousers in the same family of color, finished with a single long pendant that continues the vertical line even further down.

Picture standing at a party in that single unbroken column of deep emerald, turtleneck to trouser hem, and noticing someone glance twice, not quite placing why you suddenly seem to be the tallest woman in the room.
