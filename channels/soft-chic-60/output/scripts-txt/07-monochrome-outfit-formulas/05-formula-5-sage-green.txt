The fifth formula is soft sage green, a pale, cool-toned green rather than anything bright or grassy. This particular tone does something quietly useful for a lot of women in their fifties and beyond, it calms redness in the skin instead of drawing attention to it, because cool green sits directly opposite red on the color wheel and neutralizes it on contact, the same logic behind green-tinted skincare, just worn as clothing instead.

For work, sage trousers with a crisp white blazer keep it fresh and professional. For a day out, the same sage in a relaxed silhouette with sneakers feels easy and current. For evening, reach for a slightly deeper sage and let metallics do the shining, silver or gold both work against this shade. Because sage runs cool, balance it with a beige bag rather than anything stark, which warms the whole look up just enough to keep it from feeling clinical.

Picture wearing this on a day your skin has felt a little flushed and uneven, and noticing in every mirror you pass that your complexion looks calmer, evener, and rested, without a drop of makeup doing the work.
