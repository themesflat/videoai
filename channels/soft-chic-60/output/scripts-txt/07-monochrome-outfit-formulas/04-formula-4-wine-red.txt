The fourth formula is a deep wine red, worn head to toe, and it might be the single most flattering color on this whole list for skin that's lost a little of its natural flush over the years. Wine carries warmth without the brightness of true red, so instead of overpowering your face, it feeds color back into it, the same trick a good blush does, just amplified across an entire outfit. It belongs to fall and winter, when your skin tends to need that warmth most.

For work, ground it with a black blazer over wine trousers or a wine dress, letting the black keep things office-appropriate. For a day out, wine with denim feels unexpected and current rather than formal. For evening, go fully wine, top to bottom, and add gold jewelry, which sits beautifully against that deep red undertone. The finishing touch costs nothing extra, a lipstick in a matching wine tone ties your whole face back into the outfit.

Picture catching your reflection in a restaurant window on a cold evening, wine red from collar to boot, and realizing you look warmer and more alive than you have in months.
