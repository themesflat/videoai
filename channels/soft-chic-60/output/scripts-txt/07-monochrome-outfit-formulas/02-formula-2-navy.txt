The second formula is navy from head to toe, and if you've been reaching for black out of habit, this one might change your mind. Black under daylight can sit harshly against graying or silver hair, drawing out every shadow it creates, while navy carries just enough warmth to soften that same contrast without losing an ounce of the sophistication black is known for. It's the color stylists quietly swap in for black on camera for exactly this reason.

For work, navy trousers with a navy blazer and leather shoes look sharp enough for any meeting. For a day out, trade the blazer for a soft navy sweater and white sneakers, which keeps the look current without trying too hard. For evening, a navy dress or navy separates with silver jewelry instead of gold lets the whole outfit gleam rather than compete. Finish it with a watch that has a silver face, small, simple, and it becomes the one piece of metal your eye actually lands on.

Picture yourself walking into a dinner in unbroken navy, the silver watch catching candlelight, and realizing you look put together without a single loud choice in the whole outfit.
