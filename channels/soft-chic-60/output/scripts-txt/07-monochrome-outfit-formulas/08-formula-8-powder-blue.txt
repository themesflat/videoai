The eighth and final formula is powder blue, a pale, cool baby blue rather than anything bold or nautical. This soft a blue does something specific under harsh light, indoor fluorescents, bright afternoon sun, it diffuses rather than sharpens, gently softening fine lines the way a good filter does, and it flatters rosy or warm undertones especially well by cooling them just slightly without draining any warmth from your face.

For work, powder blue trousers with a crisp white blazer feel fresh and unexpected in a sea of navy and black. For a day out, the same blue in a relaxed fit with white sneakers reads soft and current. For evening, choose a slightly deeper powder blue and pair it with silver rather than gold, which keeps the coolness of the tone consistent from fabric to metal. Finish it with small pearl earrings, understated enough not to compete, luminous enough to catch light every time you turn your head.

Picture wearing this into a bright, sunlit room where most colors would look harsh under that light, and instead looking soft, rested, and unmistakably put together from the moment you walk in.
