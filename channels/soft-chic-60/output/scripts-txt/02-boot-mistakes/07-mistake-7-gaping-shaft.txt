The seventh and final habit is wearing a boot shaft that gaps loosely away from your calf instead of following its shape, something that happens easily as calf width and shape shift over the years. Even a beautifully made, expensive boot looks unfinished and sloppy when there's a visible gap flapping at the top, because the eye reads "doesn't fit" before it reads anything else about the outfit.

A boot with an adjustable strap or gore panel, or simply tucking a slim pant leg into the gap, closes that space and makes the fit look intentional again. Imagine walking through your whole day without once reaching down to adjust or tug at your boot shaft, simply because it was sitting close to your leg exactly the way it was designed to.
