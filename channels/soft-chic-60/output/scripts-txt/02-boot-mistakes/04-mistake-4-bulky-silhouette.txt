The fourth mistake happens when every single piece works against you at once: a chunky boot, a puffer coat, and an oversized bag, all in the same outfit, all adding volume with nothing to balance it. On their own, each piece is fine, even fashionable. Together, they stack into one shapeless silhouette that hides your frame entirely instead of skimming it, and after sixty, that stacked bulk reads as heavier than any single piece would on its own.

The fix is choosing just one bulky element to keep and paring the rest down around it. Keep the chunky boot, but pick a slimmer coat and a smaller bag, or keep the puffer coat and switch to a sleek, low-profile boot instead. Picture the exact same boots you already reached for, just paired with one slimmer piece elsewhere, and suddenly the whole outfit has shape again instead of just volume.
