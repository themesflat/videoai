You slide on your favorite pair of boots before a fall walk, feeling instantly put together in that particular way boots always seem to promise, and for a moment everything feels right. Then you catch a photo someone snapped of you crossing the street, or a reflection in a shop window at just the wrong angle, and something about your legs looks heavier and shorter than you expected, though the boots themselves are perfectly nice, and nothing else about your outfit has changed.

This isn't about buying new boots. It's about seven small, completely ordinary habits hiding in the way you're already wearing the ones you own, habits so easy to miss that most women walk right past them every single day. Once you see these seven things, you'll never reach for your boots the same way again, and every fix here takes less than a minute using things you likely already own.
