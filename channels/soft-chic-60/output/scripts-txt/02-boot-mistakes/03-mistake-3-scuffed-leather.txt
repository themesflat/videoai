The third habit is one nobody notices they're doing, because it happens through neglect rather than choice: wearing boots with dull, scuffed leather that hasn't been conditioned or polished in months, sometimes years. Leather ages the way skin does — it needs a little care to keep its richness — and dull, dry leather quietly drags down even a genuinely expensive, well-fitted outfit, making the whole look feel tired before anyone even notices why.

A five-minute pass with a leather conditioner or polish, done once every few weeks, brings back a depth and shine that photographs and looks noticeably richer than the product's price alone would suggest. Imagine pulling out boots you'd almost written off as "too worn to wear out," giving them one quick conditioning pass, and watching them look freshly bought again for the cost of a small tin of polish.
