The second mistake shows up the moment a wide-leg or slightly flared pant meets the top of a boot without any plan for where the fabric should go. Left to its own devices, that extra fabric bunches and folds right at the ankle, creating a bulky, unfinished-looking lump exactly where you'd want the leg to look longest and cleanest. It's a small detail, but it's one of the first things the eye catches, because it interrupts an otherwise smooth line from hip to floor.

The fix is about intention, not a new wardrobe. Either choose a straighter, slimmer pant that skims the boot shaft without excess fabric, or fully tuck a skinny pant into the boot for a deliberate, clean silhouette. Picture the same boots, the same weather, just a slimmer pant leg tucked neatly in, and suddenly your whole outfit looks like it was planned instead of thrown together on the way out the door.
