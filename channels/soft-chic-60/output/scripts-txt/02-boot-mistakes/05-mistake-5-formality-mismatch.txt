The fifth habit is reaching for the same casual, hiking-style boot no matter what you're wearing over it, even on days when the rest of the outfit is dressier — a flowing skirt, a tailored dress, a soft blouse. The mismatch is subtle but it registers instantly: chunky treads and rugged hardware under something elegant reads as unfinished, like you got dressed in two different moods and never reconciled them.

Keeping just one "dressy boot" in rotation — smooth leather, a subtler sole, maybe a small heel — solves this without adding a whole new category to your closet. Imagine the same skirt or dress you already love, worn once with the everyday hiking boot and once with the smoother dress boot, and noticing how only one version actually looks like it was chosen on purpose.
