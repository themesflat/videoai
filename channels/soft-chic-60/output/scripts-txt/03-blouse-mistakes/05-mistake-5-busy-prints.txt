The fifth habit is choosing a bold, busy print for a blouse without considering that it sits directly beneath your face, the one spot on your whole body where the eye naturally goes first. A loud, competing pattern pulls attention to texture and noise instead of to your eyes and your expression, and it can visually overwhelm rather than flatter, no matter how much you love the pattern itself.

Saving bold prints for a scarf, a bag, or an accessory a little further from the face, and choosing a solid color or a small, quiet print for the blouse itself, lets your face stay the focal point it should be. Imagine the same busy pattern you love, worn instead as a scarf draped over a plain blouse, and noticing how much calmer and more put-together the whole look feels.
