The fourth mistake is a sleeve that lands right at the widest part of the forearm, exactly where the fabric hem creates a small horizontal line that visually shortens and widens the arm without your realizing why the sleeve never quite feels flattering. It's rarely the arm itself that's the problem — it's the hem sitting in the one spot that draws attention to width instead of length.

Choosing a three-quarter sleeve pushed slightly higher on the forearm, or a full long sleeve instead, moves that hemline off the widest point and elongates the whole arm. Picture the same blouse with the sleeve simply pushed an inch higher, and notice how much longer and leaner your arm suddenly looks in exactly the same fabric.
