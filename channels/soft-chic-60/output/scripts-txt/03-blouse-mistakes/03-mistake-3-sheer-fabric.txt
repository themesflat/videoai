The third habit is wearing a lightweight, slightly sheer blouse with whatever happens to be underneath, a white bra, a mismatched cami, sometimes nothing at all, without realizing how visible that becomes the moment you step into daylight or under a bright overhead light. What was meant to look breezy and elegant instead reads careless the second a seam, a strap, or a bra outline shows through, and it undercuts an outfit that was otherwise entirely put together, no matter how expensive the blouse itself was.

A seamless camisole in a tone matched closely to your own skin, worn underneath, disappears completely and lets the sheer fabric do exactly what it was designed to do: look soft and light rather than accidentally revealing. For blouses in warmer tones like cream or blush, a camisole one shade deeper than your actual skin tone photographs more seamlessly than pure white, and for darker sheer blouses, a nude-toned cami still works better than black, which can show through as a visible shadow.

Imagine catching your reflection in strong sunlight outside a café, the exact moment you'd normally worry, and feeling nothing but confident, because you already know there's nothing to accidentally reveal underneath, and the blouse simply gets to look as soft as it was always meant to.
