The third habit is wearing a lightweight, slightly sheer blouse with whatever happens to be underneath, a white bra, a mismatched cami, sometimes nothing at all, without realizing how visible that becomes in daylight. What was meant to look breezy and elegant instead reads careless the moment a seam or strap shows through, and it undercuts an outfit that was otherwise entirely put together.

A seamless camisole in a tone matched closely to your own skin, worn underneath, disappears completely and lets the sheer fabric do exactly what it was designed to do: look soft and light rather than accidentally revealing. Imagine catching your reflection in strong sunlight and feeling nothing but confident, because you already know there's nothing to worry about underneath.
