The second mistake is buttoning a blouse all the way to the very top button, the way we were taught looked "proper," without noticing what it does to the face. A collar closed tight at the throat boxes in the neckline and reads prim and severe rather than soft, and it can make even a relaxed, friendly expression look a little more guarded and a little older than it is.

Undoing just the top one or two buttons, or reaching for a blouse with a soft V or gentle scoop neckline in the first place, opens up the whole face instantly. Picture the same blouse, the same morning, with just one button left open, and notice how much softer and more approachable your whole expression suddenly looks in the mirror.
