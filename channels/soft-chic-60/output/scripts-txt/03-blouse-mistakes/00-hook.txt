You put on a nice blouse for lunch with friends, the one that always makes you feel a little more finished, and leaving the house you genuinely feel good. Then somewhere between the second coffee and the walk back to the car, you catch yourself in a shop window and something reads tired, a little heavier around the middle, a little older around the face, even though nothing has actually happened to the blouse itself.

This isn't about buying a new blouse. It's about six small habits hiding in the way you're already wearing the ones you own, habits so ordinary you'd never think to question them. Once you see these six things, you'll never reach for a blouse the same way again, and every fix here takes less than a minute using things you likely already own.
