The sixth and final mistake is reaching for a blouse in a fabric that looks beautiful ironed at eight in the morning and creased and tired by noon, usually a pure cotton or pure linen with nothing else blended in. The wrinkling itself isn't really the issue — it's what it quietly does to the impression you left that morning, undoing all the effort you put in before anyone even sees you again in the afternoon.

A cotton-poly or silk blend blouse holds its shape and stays crisp through a full day without any extra effort on your part, so the put-together woman who left the house at nine is still the one people see at three. Imagine wearing the exact same style of blouse, just in a blend instead of pure cotton, and catching your reflection at the end of a long day looking every bit as fresh as you did at breakfast.
