The second jean to own is a high-rise wide-leg, and it solves a problem most women over 60 have quietly been fighting with the wrong tools. As the midsection naturally softens with age, a low or mid-rise waistband tends to sit right at the widest, least flattering point, creating a visible line that draws the eye exactly where you don't want it. A proper high rise sits above that point entirely, skimming over rather than cutting across, while the wide leg balances the silhouette so the whole look reads as intentional and proportioned rather than simply loose.

The trick to wearing this jean well is tucking in a fitted or semi-fitted top, never a loose one, so the waist definition the high rise gives you actually gets to show. For work, tuck in a fine-knit sweater and add a structured blazer for a look that feels current without trying too hard. For a weekend outing, a fitted white tee tucked in with a denim jacket over the top feels effortless. And for evening, a silky camisole tucked into the wide leg with a statement earring turns the same pair into something dressier entirely.

Picture catching your reflection in a shop window on your way to lunch, waist defined, leg falling in an easy wide line to the floor, and realizing the outfit that's turning heads today is built around the exact same denim you'd normally reach past on the rack.
