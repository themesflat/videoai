The sixth jean worth having is a white or soft ivory pair, and it's the one color of denim most women over 60 quietly rule out, assuming it belongs only to summer or to women twenty years younger. In reality, a pale denim does something no dark wash can, it brightens everything near your face and hands by reflecting light back upward, which is especially flattering as skin tone softens and evens out with age. Worn into early autumn against richer, deeper colors, white denim reads as confident and current rather than out of season.

The key to wearing pale denim well after 60 is pairing it with texture and depth rather than more brightness. For the office, a chocolate brown or forest green sweater over white jeans creates immediate contrast that looks deliberate. For a weekend outing, a chunky cream cardigan and tan boots keep the palette soft and autumnal. And for an evening event, a deep burgundy blouse with the white jean and gold jewelry turns it into a genuinely elegant statement piece rather than an afterthought.

Picture reaching past the white jeans in your closet all September, assuming they're only for summer, then finally pairing them with a rich burgundy sweater one October evening and catching your own reflection looking brighter and more awake than you have in weeks.
