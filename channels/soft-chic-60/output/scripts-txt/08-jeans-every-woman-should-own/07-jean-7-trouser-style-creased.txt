The seventh and final jean every woman over 60 should own is a trouser-style jean with a pressed center crease, and it might do more to elevate a look than any other pair on this list. The pressed crease running down the front of each leg is a detail borrowed entirely from tailored trousers, and it visually reads as dress pants long before anyone realizes they're looking at denim at all. This is the jean for moments when jeans themselves feel slightly too casual, but a full suit or formal trouser feels like more than the occasion calls for.

Because it already looks dressed up, this jean asks for very little effort elsewhere. For the office or an important meeting, pair it with a silk blouse and pointed flats, and it will comfortably stand in for tailored trousers without anyone questioning it. For a smart weekend lunch, a soft blazer and loafers keep it polished but relaxed. And for an evening event, add heels and a fitted blouse, and the crease alone carries the whole look into genuinely dressy territory.

Picture walking into a nice restaurant for a friend's birthday dinner, having thrown this jean on in under two minutes with a blouse you already owned, and having someone ask, with real surprise in their voice, whether those are actually trousers.
