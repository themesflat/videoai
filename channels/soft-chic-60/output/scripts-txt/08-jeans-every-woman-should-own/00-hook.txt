You're standing in a fitting room holding the exact same jean you bought five years ago, the one that used to feel like your best-kept secret, and something about it just isn't working the way it used to. Here's what nobody selling you jeans ever explains: the cut of a jean changes where the eye lands on your body, and after 60, where the eye lands matters more than it ever has. A dark straight leg reads completely differently than a soft bootcut, and a crisp trouser-style jean can carry you into rooms a weekend pair never could. Owning the right handful, not more jeans, just the right ones, means you're never standing in front of your closet on a Tuesday morning wondering what on earth to wear.
