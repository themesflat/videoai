The fourth mistake has nothing to do with style at all and everything to do with fit: nose pads that were set once, years ago, and never touched since. As we age, the cartilage and skin along the bridge of the nose change subtly, becoming a little softer and less able to hold a frame exactly where it was originally adjusted to sit, which is why a pair of glasses that used to stay perfectly in place now slides down within the hour, leaving you pushing them up over and over throughout the day and often leaving faint red marks or indentations on either side of your nose by evening.

The fix is resisting the urge to bend the pads yourself with your fingers, which almost always makes the fit uneven rather than better, and instead having them professionally adjusted by an optician every six months, a five-minute appointment that costs nothing at most shops and instantly solves a problem you may have simply been living with for years without questioning it. This one applies to every single occasion equally, because it isn't about which frame looks best where, it's about the foundation underneath all of it, and no frame, however flattering the shape or the color, can look elegant while it's sliding down your nose or leaving marks you then have to quietly cover with makeup.

Picture sitting across from someone at dinner, pushing your glasses up for the third time in ten minutes without even registering you're doing it anymore, it's become that automatic, and then picture the same dinner three days after a proper adjustment, your glasses simply staying put, leaving you free to actually be present in the conversation instead of quietly managing your own face the whole time it lasts.
