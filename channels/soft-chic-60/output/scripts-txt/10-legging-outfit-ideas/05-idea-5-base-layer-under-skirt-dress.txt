The fifth idea is using leggings as a hidden base layer underneath a skirt or a dress once the weather turns cold, rather than wearing them visibly at all. This one is less about styling the leggings and more about letting them disappear entirely, so all the warmth of a legging comes through with none of the risk of the outfit ever reading as workout wear, because from the outside, nobody sees leggings at all, only a skirt or dress that happens to keep you warm.

At the office, a thin pair of ponte leggings worn under a wool skirt or dress, finished with knee-high boots, keeps the legs warm through the coldest months without changing the silhouette of the outfit one bit. Out for the day, leggings under a denim skirt with sneakers feels casual and entirely intentional. For an evening event outdoors, opaque leggings worn under a slip dress with heels mean you can stay out in the cold comfortably while looking exactly as elegant as the dress alone would suggest.
