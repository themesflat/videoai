The fourth idea is choosing a cropped, cuffed-hem legging, pairing it with loafers, and finishing with a cropped sweater on top. Full-length leggings worn head to toe in one unbroken line of fabric are exactly what reads as "athletic loungewear" from across a room, but the moment the ankle shows, with a cuffed hem and a proper shoe rather than a sneaker, the outfit suddenly signals that it was styled on purpose rather than pulled straight from a gym bag.

At the office, cropped leggings with loafers and a cropped merino sweater feel polished without a single formal piece involved. Out running errands, the same cropped leggings with canvas loafers and a cropped cardigan look easy without slipping into sloppy. For evening, swap in a pair of patent loafers or a small kitten heel, add a fitted cropped sweater and a delicate necklace, and the ankle becomes the most elegant detail in the entire outfit.

Picture showing a little ankle in a pair of loafers at a weekend brunch, and watching someone assume you got properly dressed up for the occasion, when really it only took a cuffed hem and the right shoe.
