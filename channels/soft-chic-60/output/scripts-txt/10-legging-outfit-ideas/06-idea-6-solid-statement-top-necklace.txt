The sixth idea is keeping the bottom half deliberately simple, a solid dark legging with no pattern or shine to it, and letting a statement top and a long necklace carry all the elegance instead. Trying to make the legging itself the interesting part of the outfit is where most of the "sloppy" feeling actually comes from. Once the bottom half stops competing for attention, a genuinely beautiful top and a piece of jewelry that catches the light are free to do the one job they do best, pulling every eye upward toward your face.

For the office, dark solid leggings under a silk blouse with a long pendant necklace layered over it look considered and polished with almost no effort. Out running errands or meeting friends, the same dark leggings under a printed statement tee with two or three layered necklaces feel current without trying too hard. For evening, swap in a sequin or velvet top over the same simple leggings, add one long necklace, and the whole outfit suddenly belongs at a dinner table rather than a living room.

Picture the compliment landing entirely on the necklace and the top at a dinner with friends, nobody thinking to glance twice at what's underneath, because the leggings were never meant to be noticed in the first place.
