The second idea is swapping thin cotton-knit leggings for a pair in ponte or faux leather, and then layering a blazer on top. Thin, soft leggings cling and wrinkle in a way that reads instantly as loungewear, no matter what's worn with them, because the fabric itself telegraphs "sweatpants" before anyone even registers the outfit. Ponte and faux leather hold their shape the way a trouser does, so the same silhouette suddenly reads as tailored rather than borrowed from the gym bag, and a blazer on top finishes the illusion completely.

At the office, ponte leggings under a tailored blazer with a pair of loafers look almost indistinguishable from a proper trouser suit from a few feet away. Out running errands, faux-leather leggings under an oversized blazer with clean white sneakers feel current and deliberate rather than athletic. For evening, faux-leather leggings paired with a fitted velvet blazer and a low heel move the whole look straight into "going out" territory, no actual pants required.

Picture pulling on a pair of ponte leggings and your favorite blazer for a casual coffee with a friend, and having her ask, genuinely curious, what brand those "pants" are, never once suspecting they're leggings at all.
