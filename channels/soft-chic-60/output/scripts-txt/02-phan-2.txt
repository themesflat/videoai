The second habit lives right at your collarbone, in a place most of us never think to examine: the neckline. A high, snug crew neck feels cozy, but it does something specific to where people look first in any conversation, drawing the eye straight to the neck and jawline, which happen to be two of the earliest places time leaves its mark. It isn't that the crew neck is unflattering on its own. It's that it points a spotlight exactly where you'd rather not have one.

A gentle V-neck, or a wider boat neck that skims just off the shoulder, changes the entire conversation your outfit is having. Instead of framing the neck tightly, it opens the space around it, softening the transition from jaw to collarbone and letting a simple necklace or a pair of earrings do quiet, elegant work. Women who make this one switch often say the same thing: someone asks if they've done something different, changed their hair, changed their skincare, when really the only thing that changed was two inches of fabric near the collar. It's a small adjustment with an outsized effect, and it costs nothing beyond noticing which necklines you already own and reaching for the right one more often.
