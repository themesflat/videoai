The fourth trick folds a scarf into a band and ties it back like a headband, sitting right along the hairline, tails knotted and tucked or left loose at the nape depending on your mood. Fold a square scarf into a long strip, or use a rectangular one as is, position it just above your hairline, and tie it snugly at the back.

Where this one earns its place is right along the hairline and cheekbones, two of the most visible spots on your whole face. A scarf folded into a band covers any unruly roots, regrowth, or greys right at the part line, the exact spot most people glance at first without even realizing it, and it does something else too, it draws a clean horizontal line just above your brow that subtly lifts the whole face, drawing attention up toward your cheekbones and eyes rather than down.

For work, a solid minimalist fold in a quiet tone looks intentional and polished rather than casual. Heading out for the day, a bold-print fold becomes the statement piece of the whole outfit, the kind of thing people notice before anything else you're wearing. And for morning errands or a walk, swap silk for a light cotton scarf instead, easier to tie snugly and far more comfortable against a warm hairline than slippery silk. Add a pair of statement earrings to balance the fold, since the headband draws so much attention upward that your ears deserve a little something to match.

Imagine running into an old coworker at the grocery store on a day you didn't even bother washing your hair, scarf folded and tied back in under a minute, and she tells you your skin looks incredible today. It wasn't your skin. It was one folded scarf lifting everything above your shoulders, doing quietly in a minute what a salon visit takes an hour to do.
